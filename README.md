## 🛠️ Sugar Island Volunteer IT Help Desk

**Role:** Founder & Volunteer Technician
**Status:** Active, started about 4 months ago
**Type:** Free, community-based, on-site tech support

I started a free help desk for island residents who need hands-on help with everyday technology. Because it's a small community, I work one-on-one and often return for follow-up visits.

### Work Completed (anonymized)

**Case 1: Mobile phone recovery and setup**
- Replaced and activated a lost SIM card in a Tracfone
- Streamlined the home screens and enabled accessibility features
- Recovered a lost account password
- Set up a new phone for online bill payment
- Follow-up visit: walked the user through canceling a Microsoft 365 subscription

**Case 2: Home internet troubleshooting (5 visits)**
- Visit 1: Confirmed the internet service was working and showed the user how to log on
- Visit 2: Troubleshot a Starlink password error. Nobody knew who set up the account or who paid the bill, so I used a portable mobile hotspot to get them online while they sorted out account ownership
- Visit 3: Installed and configured a smart TV
- Final visit: Retrieved my equipment before they left the island for the winter

### Skills Demonstrated
- Hands-on troubleshooting
- Account and password recovery
- Mobile device configuration and accessibility settings
- Home network and satellite internet (Starlink) support
- Smart TV setup
- Clear communication with non-technical users
- Follow-up and customer retention (repeat clients)
- Protecting client privacy

### Privacy
Client names, credentials, account details and screenshots are never shared or stored here.
