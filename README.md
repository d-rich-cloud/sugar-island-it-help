## 🛠️ Sugar Island Volunteer IT Help Desk

**Role:** Founder & Volunteer Technician
**Status:** Active, started about 4 months ago
**Type:** Free, community-based, on-site tech support

I run a free help desk for island residents and local organizations. Because it's a small community, I work one-on-one, make follow-up visits, and match solutions to people's budgets.

### Work Completed (anonymized)

**Case 1: Mobile phone recovery and setup**
- Replaced and activated a lost SIM card in a Tracfone
- Streamlined screens and enabled accessibility features
- Recovered a lost account password
- Set up a new phone for online bill payment
- Follow-up: walked the user through canceling a Microsoft 365 subscription

**Case 2: Home internet troubleshooting (5 visits)**
- Confirmed the internet service was working and showed the user how to log on
- Troubleshot a Starlink password error. Nobody knew who set up the account or paid the bill, so I used a portable hotspot to get them online while they sorted out account ownership
- Installed and configured a smart TV
- Retrieved my equipment before they left the island for the winter

**Case 3: Slow computer, upgrade and Linux migration**
- Diagnosed a slow all-in-one PC and recommended the cheapest fix: an 8 GB RAM upgrade (to 16 GB) plus a cleanup of startup and background processes
- The user wanted to do the install herself, so I coached her through it
- A cable was partially unplugged during reassembly, and a repair attempt damaged the LCD. We didn't find out until later
- Took the PC home to work on it, then set up a spare 16 GB HP laptop with Ubuntu
- Gave her a hands-on crash course and told her to try to break it. Within a day she was using Linux for her daily tasks
- Sourcing an affordable replacement screen
- In progress: setting up Ubuntu dual boot on her all-in-one, using HDMI as the primary display, so she has a working PC again in the meantime

**Case 4: Community organization (town shop and winter shelter)**
- Researched, purchased and set up a smart TV for staff training
- The TV also provides entertainment for children during emergency shelter use

**Case 5: Local business point-of-sale support**
- Restored a point-of-sale tablet system that went down mid-shift
- Reinstalled printers so staff could print receipts again

### Skills Demonstrated
- Hands-on troubleshooting under time pressure
- Hardware upgrades and diagnostics
- Ubuntu and Linux setup, including dual boot
- Account and password recovery
- Mobile device configuration and accessibility
- Home networking and satellite internet (Starlink)
- Budget-conscious purchasing and recommendations
- Teaching non-technical users
- Client privacy and follow-through

### Lessons Learned
- Let users do hands-on work when they want to, but check every connector and test the device before powering it up
- Start a repair with a quick hardware inspection and a before/after check
- A spare laptop with a friendly OS can be a great stopgap solution

### Privacy
Client names, credentials, account details and screenshots are never shared or stored here.
